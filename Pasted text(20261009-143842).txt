# AUTHORITATIVE PROJECT UPDATE — 09 NOVEMBER 2026

## Change-control notice

This update records the subsequently approved production rules and hybrid-strategy clarifications. It supersedes any older, conflicting wording in the original sections below. The original V8.0 specification is retained for traceability; do not interpret superseded examples or thresholds as active production rules. The current implementation must also be checked against the latest code and the persistent working register `NSE_V8_HYBRID_STRATEGY_MASTER_PARAMETERS_2026-11-09.md`.

### A. Universe and historical data — current rule

1. Use eligible NSE Mainboard Largecap, Midcap and Smallcap securities.
2. Exclude SME securities, ETFs and Mutual Funds.
3. Reuse the available approximately 200 complete, distinct NSE trading-session Bhavcopy history/cache. Do not redownload valid cached sessions unnecessarily.
4. Use the official NSE trading calendar, including notified special sessions. Missing or invalid data for an expected session is `WAIT_FOR_DATA`.
5. No Top-20 or Top-50 shortlist cap is permitted.
6. The old `Price > ₹50` example is not an active hard rejection rule unless separately approved and documented after backtesting.

### B. Authoritative Delivery A/B screening rule — clarified 10 November 2026

1. The Delivery window is the 30 calendar days ending on and including the EOD as-of date.
2. Count **all** valid, distinct, candidate-specific NSE Delivery report sessions inside this 30-calendar-day window, and never use a report dated after the as-of date. Do not restrict the production calculation to the latest five sessions.
3. **Minimum data requirement:** at least five valid distinct report sessions are required. With fewer than five, set `WAIT_FOR_DATA`; do not PASS or FAIL the stock on an incomplete window.
4. If more than five valid sessions exist, use **every valid session available in the 30-day window** for both Category A and Category B. Do not stop at five and do not discard older in-window valid sessions merely to create a five-day sample.
5. **Category A — Average Delivery Strength:** calculate the arithmetic average Delivery Percentage across all valid sessions in the 30-day window. PASS when the average is `>=60.00%`; exactly 60.00% passes. A daily value below 60.00% does not by itself fail Category A if the full-window average remains >=60.00%.
6. **Category B — Consistent Daily Delivery Strength:** PASS only when every valid session in the same 30-day window has Delivery Percentage `>=60.00%`. Exactly 60.00% passes. One valid session below 60.00% means Category B FAIL, while Category A must still be evaluated independently.
7. Evaluate A and B independently. The eligible cohort is the full `A OR B` union. Preserve `A_ONLY`, `B_ONLY` (should not normally occur when A is computed as the same-window arithmetic average and threshold), and `A_AND_B` membership/status; de-duplicate by symbol. Category B is the stronger consistency classification; it is not a separate BUY signal.
8. **Required result/report fields per stock:** as-of date; 30-calendar-day window start/end; valid Delivery session count; expected/available coverage where determinable; each valid session date and Delivery %; arithmetic average Delivery %; minimum daily Delivery %; maximum daily Delivery %; Category A status; Category B status; membership (`A_ONLY` / `A_AND_B`); and reason/status (`PASS` / `FAIL` / `WAIT_FOR_DATA`). Show the actual number of valid sessions used (for example, 5/30, 12/30 or 20/30) so a reader can distinguish a five-session sample from a broader sample. Do not imply that 30 reports are expected: 30 is a calendar-day window, not 30 trading sessions.
9. Keep existing 5D/10D/20D consistency fields as additional observations only; they must not replace or alter the 30-calendar-day A/B eligibility rule.
10. A requested separate five-session delivery-only YAML/watchlist remains a separate artifact and must not be confused with the production 30-calendar-day A/B pipeline.

### C. Authoritative execution order — Delivery before RVOL

1. Load and validate the latest NSE EOD Bhavcopy; build the eligible Mainboard universe.
2. Reuse prior 20 complete, distinct trading sessions and prepare the per-symbol average-volume baseline only. Do not calculate RVOL for the entire Mainboard universe at this point.
3. Apply Delivery A and B independently, then build the complete de-duplicated A OR B union.
4. Only after that union exists, calculate each candidate's RVOL as current EOD traded volume divided by the average volume of the previous 20 complete sessions. Exclude the current session from the baseline. Require exactly 20 valid prior sessions; do not silently substitute older sessions for missing/duplicate/invalid sessions.
5. Classify `RVOL > 1.5` as `HIGH_RVOL_GT_1_5`; classify `RVOL <= 1.5` as `LOW_RVOL_LE_1_5`; missing/invalid inputs are `WAIT_FOR_DATA`.
6. RVOL is an informational classification, not an eligibility rejection gate. Every A/B candidate — HIGH, LOW or WAIT_FOR_DATA — continues to the existing Liquidity stage. No Top-20/Top-50 RVOL cap.
7. Preserve RVOL and the verified 20-session baseline downstream; do not recalculate RVOL for the full universe or a later cohort.

### D. Liquidity — current approved threshold

1. The existing hard minimum is 20-session average daily traded value `>=₹1.5 crore`.
2. Descriptive tiers: STRONG `>=₹5 crore/day`; MEDIUM `>=₹2 crore and <₹5 crore/day`; LOW `>=₹1.5 crore and <₹2 crore/day`.
3. ₹5 crore is not the hard minimum. The older ₹2 crore hard-gate wording is superseded. Do not raise the ₹1.5 crore hard minimum without explicit approval.
4. A below-₹1.5 crore shadow list is not an active production path; it remains a pending decision.

### E. Fundamentals and cache — current handling

1. Fundamental metrics enrich classification, priority and risk context; they are not an early rejection gate.
2. Do not apply a Strong Fundamental gate before collection. Missing metrics remain visible with explicit statuses; do not silently drop candidates because a metric is missing.
3. Persist the Fundamental Cache inside this GitHub repository, reuse it across runs and retain historical snapshots and official-verification status. Do not commit credentials or secrets.
4. Official filing verification must match exact symbol, exact period-end and valid publication/as-of timing. Unresolved values remain unverified / `WAIT_FOR_DATA`; never infer missing dates or substitute a different period.
5. The current code collects fundamentals after Liquidity. Whether to move collection before Liquidity and collect every A/B candidate is a pending decision; do not change this boundary until explicitly approved.
6. A provider returning data (`DATA_FOUND`) is not proof that the report is complete or officially verified.

### F. RSI, Delivery Quantity and accumulation evidence

1. RSI is informational only. It must not reject a stock, reduce its eligibility or apply a score penalty.
2. Delivery Quantity, Delivery Percentage and total traded volume must be interpreted together. High Delivery Percentage alone does not prove accumulation or identify the buyer.
3. Use the label `Potential Accumulation Evidence`; do not claim `Institutional Accumulation Confirmed` from NSE Delivery data.
4. Delivery Quantity trend, price response/stability, selling-pressure context, base-range measurements and support/resistance observations are evidence/reporting fields unless a separate approved gate explicitly says otherwise.
5. Preserve raw values, source dates, calculation windows, duplicate/missing-data reasons and `WAIT_FOR_DATA` statuses.

### G. Hybrid observations and watchlists

Maintain four independent outputs:
1. `Potential Accumulation`
2. `Base / VCP Forming`
3. `Breakout Ready`
4. `Confirmed Entry`

Potential Accumulation and Base/VCP observations must not be removed merely because a later breakout/entry confirmation is absent. Early-stage watchlists are not BUY signals and must not bypass existing V8 confirmation or risk controls.

Observe fixed-range base high/low/width/duration, repeated support/resistance behavior, Higher Low / Double Bottom, contraction depth, range narrowing, volume dry-up, EMA20/EMA44/EMA50/EMA200 context, breakout follow-through and post-breakout retest quality. The current three-block VCP/rolling-window measurements and 10-trading-session post-breakout follow-through report are descriptive observations only unless a threshold is explicitly approved.

Do not invent or hard-code pending values for VCP contraction count/depth, base width/duration, Pocket Pivot formula/lookback/candle conditions, false-breakout buffer, retest window, or support/resistance zone tolerance. Pocket Pivot remains unapproved until its exact formula is decided. A volume dry-up ratio below 0.5x may be displayed as an observational flag only; it is not a rejection gate.

EMA20/EMA44/EMA50/EMA200 values and distances are observations. Do not automatically reject an early accumulation candidate merely because price is below EMA44, EMA50 or EMA200. Do not change the existing EMA44 production gate without explicit approval.

### H. Preserve later V8 confirmations and risk controls

Keep the existing Sector Strength, verified News/Catalyst, mandatory 3+1 confirmation, structural stop-loss, minimum 2R risk/reward, Chase filter, Risk Control and position-sizing logic. Do not weaken or remove these rules while implementing earlier hybrid observations. Missing required evidence remains `WAIT_FOR_DATA`.

Calculate risk/reward from real, verified structural levels. Never synthesize a target or default 2R value when a verified resistance/target level is unavailable. Market-wide FII/DII flow is contextual and does not prove stock-specific institutional buying.

# 46. MOST IMPORTANT RULES

### RULE 1

Volume Gainer method MUST remain.

### RULE 2

Volume Gainer is a SCREENING method, not a final BUY signal.

### RULE 3

EOD scan should run after NSE Bhavcopy becomes available, not at a fixed 4:00 PM.

### RULE 4

5-day volume behaviour must be checked.

### RULE 5

For accumulation/pullback setups, at least 60% of the previous 5 days should preferably have volume below the 20D average.

### RULE 6

Volume expansion must be interpreted with price action.

### RULE 7

Always determine WHY volume increased.

### RULE 8

High volume alone is never a BUY signal.

### RULE 9

3+1 confirmation is mandatory.

### RULE 10

Same news from multiple websites counts as one confirmation.

### RULE 11

Score alone cannot create an entry.

### RULE 12

R:R should preferably be >=1:2.

### RULE 13

Do not chase excessively extended stocks.

### RULE 14

EOD candidate must be re-confirmed next morning.

### RULE 15

Opening gap, VWAP, volume and price action must be checked before final entry.

### RULE 16

After entry, monitor the original thesis every trading day.

### RULE 17

Negative material news can invalidate a technically strong setup.

### RULE 18

Target is not the only exit condition.

### RULE 19

Capital protection and thesis invalidation take priority over waiting for target.

### RULE 20

No trade is better than a low-quality trade.

# 49. IMPORTANT MATHEMATICAL PRINCIPLE

A strategy does not need a 60%+ win rate to be profitable.

Example:

Win Rate = 45%

Average Winner = +2R

Average Loser = -1R

Expectancy:

0.45 × 2R - 0.55 × 1R

= +0.35R per trade

Therefore:

# EXPECTANCY > WIN RATE

is the primary objective.

# FINAL PRINCIPLE

The system should NOT ask:

"Which stock went up the most?"

It should ask:

"Which stock has unusual volume, WHY the volume increased, whether that activity is accumulation or distribution, whether price structure is favourable, whether sector/market support the move, whether a reliable catalyst exists, whether 3+1 confirmation is satisfied, whether the risk/reward is favourable, and whether tomorrow's market confirms the thesis?"

Only then:

# FINAL ENTRY

And after entry:

"Is my original thesis still valid?"

If YES → HOLD / TRAIL

If weakening → CAUTION

If invalidated → EXIT

This is the complete NSE Swing Trading System V8.0 specification.
