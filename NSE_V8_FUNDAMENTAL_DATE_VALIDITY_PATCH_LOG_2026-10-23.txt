NSE V8 FUNDAMENTAL DATE VALIDITY PATCH
Date: 2026-10-23

Root cause:
- Yahoo provider previously wrote the script retrieval date into AsOfDate.
- Fundamental filter interpreted AsOfDate as the public availability date, causing historical EOD runs to mark provider data as future-dated.
- nextFiscalYearEnd could be misidentified as the financial period end.

Fix:
- Add DataRetrievedAtUTC as a separate retrieval timestamp.
- Leave AsOfDate blank for Yahoo data unless a genuine publication/availability date is known.
- Report PUBLICATION_DATE_UNVERIFIED when publication date is missing.
- Do not use nextFiscalYearEnd as the financial period end.
- Flag FinancialPeriodEnd after EOD as FINANCIAL_PERIOD_END_AFTER_EOD.
- Keep collection non-blocking: no Delivery A/B candidates are rejected due to missing or unverified fundamentals.

- py_compile: PASS for fundamental_data_provider.py, fundamental_filter.py, test_fundamental_data_provider.py, test_fundamental_filter.py
- Focused pytest: 13 passed
- Live Yahoo Finance and GitHub Actions execution were not tested from this environment.
